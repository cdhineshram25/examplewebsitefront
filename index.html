Phase I — The Origin
01 / GENESIS

THE ORIGIN

A cacao pod begins the journey. Born in rich equatorial soil under dense rainforest canopies, wild Criollo and Trinitario pods mature slowly, gathering moisture, depth, and volcanic minerality through seasons of mist and tropical rains.

Latitude 4° 12' 00" S
Cultivar Criollo Ancestral
Ferment 6 Days Banana Leaf
02 / FLAME

THE ROAST

Cocoa beans develop their deep aroma and character. Gentle indirect woodfire heat coaxing forth notes of toasted hazelnut, dark espresso, and subtle wildflower honey while preserving the bean's delicate floral acids.

Heat Profile 118°C Indirect
Duration 38 Minutes
Wood Aged Olivewood
03 / CONCHE

THE TRANSFORMATION

Roasted cocoa melts into rich flowing chocolate. Ground for over seventy-two continuous hours between granite stones until microscopic velvet emulsion is achieved, balancing bitterness with natural cacao butter richness.

Stone Wheel Natural Granite
Fineness 12 Microns
Viscosity Silken Stream
04 / TEMPER

THE FINISH

Chocolate becomes a precise, beautifully crafted bar. Tempered to molecular perfection for that singular, crisp acoustic snap and warm satin sheen that yields softly to the palate in waves of lingering cacao essence.

Crystalline Form V Beta
Cacao Content 85% Grand Cru
Acoustic Sharp Snap
